Faith & Stride Ministry

Running, Walking & Fitness for Every Soul

Whether you’ve never run a step or you’re preparing for a full marathon, our running ministry is built on encouragement, mutual support, and prayer. No one runs alone!

Walking & Couch-to-5K

Gentle run-walk intervals designed for beginners, seniors, and parents with strollers. Build stamina at your own comfortable pace.

Pace: 12-16 min/mi Saturdays 7:00 AM

Community 5K Joggers

Our most popular group! Social, conversational runs through scenic trails followed by devotional reflection and coffee fellowship.

Pace: 9:30-11:30 min/mi Sat & Tue

Track & Speed Workouts

Weekly interval training at the local high school track. Improve aerobic threshold, running form, and mental perseverance.

All Levels Welcome Thursdays 6:30 PM

Endurance & Marathon Team

Structured Saturday long runs (8 to 18 miles) for half and full marathoners, raising funds for water wells and missionary projects.

Pace: 7:30-9:00 min/mi Saturdays 6:00 AM
